# 🔵 PHYLLOBYTES.

## Introduction

Hello! We want to present **Phyllobytes**.

<p>&emsp;&emsp;**With Phyllobytes we want to tackle a real problematic in our current world, as extreme rainfall patterns shift, communities, essential services, and infrastructure planners need to understand what is happening in their local areas.**</p>

<p>&emsp;&emsp;**Phyllobytes** transforms open-source NASA precipitation data into an accessible web-based atlas, allowing users, communities and municipalities to explore the entire country of Costa Rica, as well as the rest of Central America. It grasp historical trends, understand local changes and helps make better decisions using real data. **Providing knowledge to help communities prepare for the future, helping places in which this data is not analyzed nor compare.**</p>

<p>&emsp;&emsp;**Phyllobytes** is more than a weather dashboard. Originating in Costa Rica, it is a platform built on the belief that Earth observation data should be accessible to everyone. By **transforming complex environmental information into clear, meaningful insights**, we are making **knowledge more accessible** and empowering people from small communities to better understand our planet.</p>

<p>&emsp;&emsp;**We do not want people to have to understand science in order to benefit from it. We want to use science and technology so that everyone can better understand the place where they live.**</p>
